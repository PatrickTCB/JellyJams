Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the Jelly Jams app for mobile devices, together with any related services operated by Patrick Coffey (collectively, the "Application"). Patrick Coffey is hereby referred to as the "Service Provider".


Information Collection and Use

The Application collects no information whatsoever. Nothing about your or your device is collected by the applictation.

The Application connects to your Jellyfin Server and, optionally, to your instance of the Jelly Jams Backend. Both of those server applications would be under your control, and are not run by the Service Provider, me, or anyone connected to me.

Residents of many jurisdictions (eg. California, the EU) have the right to request any data held about them. In this case, I collect literally no information at all.

For inquires you can e-mail jellyjams@coffey.app.

Changes

The Service Provider, me, may need to update this Privacy Policy in the future. I expect this to mostly be adding boiler plate related to newly enacted privacy legislation in various regions, confirming that I do not collect process or transmit any information about my users.

However, in the event that I begin to track any private information, eg adding some kind of analytics or crash detections, all users will be notified well in advance of such a change and given a specific date upon which the collection of such data shall begin.

Final Summary

This is a music playing app that connects to your Jellyfin server and serves your own files back to you. Nothing is collected by me at all. Check out the source code on GitHub to see everything this app can do. Including this very policy.
